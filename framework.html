From research to allocation

PG Composite Strategy connects signal research to portfolio construction and execution. The framework gives each layer a distinct role, then evaluates their interactions through portfolio and post-trade review.

01

Observe & model

RESEARCH INPUTS

Price, volume, order flow, and market context inform higher-timeframe, CTA, multi-factor, and machine-learning research.

02

Fuse signals

WEIGHTS / FILTERS

The published model framework studies dynamic LSTM–ARIMA weighting and 14-period RSI thresholds to combine signals across horizons.

03

Construct the portfolio

ALLOCATION / EXPOSURE

Portfolio relationships, allocation constraints, and hedge research are examined together. Published BTC–BNB research uses Johansen cointegration filtering.

04

Execute & review

TWAP / VWAP

Time- and volume-weighted approaches connect target allocation to execution. Post-trade review examines liquidity, slippage, funding, and portfolio outcomes.

Validation & feedback

Observation, hypothesis, validation, and review form a continuing research process. Model outputs are assessed in the context of regime changes, parameter uncertainty, and execution conditions.

Signal validation

Examine the signal construction, sampling basis, and historical assumptions.

Portfolio review

Review drawdowns, exposure, concentration, and the interaction of portfolio inputs.

Execution evidence

Assess the reporting basis, fees, funding, and account or venue dependencies.

Model iteration

Use findings to refine the next hypothesis and validation agenda.
